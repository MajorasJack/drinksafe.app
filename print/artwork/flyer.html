Looking out for

A free, anonymous place to report drink spiking at UK venues, and to check a venue before you go.

Five things that help

  1. 1Never leave your drink unattended, even for a moment.
  2. 2Watch your drink being prepared and carried to you.
  3. 3If your drink tastes or smells unusual, do not drink it.
  4. 4Stay with trusted friends and look out for each other.
  5. 5If you feel unwell or suspect you have been spiked, tell someone immediately and seek help.

If it happens

  1. 1Tell bar staff. They can help you get somewhere safe.
  2. 2Stay with people you trust. Do not leave on your own.
  3. 3If you feel very unwell, ask staff to call for help.
