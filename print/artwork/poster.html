Drink Safe

Think your drink
has been spiked?

Often the only sign is feeling far drunker than you should for what you have had. Dizziness or confusion can also be signs.

  1. 1Tell bar staff now. They can help you get somewhere safe.
  2. 2Stay with people you trust. Do not leave on your own.
  3. 3If you feel very unwell, ask staff to call for help.
