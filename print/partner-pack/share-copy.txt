Drink Safe is a free, anonymous place to report drink spiking at UK venues, and to check a venue before you go. Search a city, see what has been reported, and read each venue's reports and concern level. If something happens to you, report it with no names and no sign-up; every report is reviewed before it is published.

If it happens: often the only sign is feeling far drunker than you should for what you have had. Tell bar staff now, stay with people you trust, and if you feel very unwell ask staff to call for help.

Who can help: 999 in an emergency, 101 for non-emergency, Crimestoppers 0800 555 111, Victim Support 08 08 16 89 111.
