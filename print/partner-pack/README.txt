Drink Safe partner pack
=======================

Thank you for helping. Everything in this pack is free to print and share as
many times as you like.

Please only put anything up with the venue's permission. Ask the manager, or
whoever looks after the building, first.


For an office printer
---------------------

drink-safe-poster-a4.pdf
drink-safe-poster-a3.pdf
    The poster, at A4 and A3. It has a white background, so it prints well in
    black and white. Good places for it are toilets, welfare, first aid and
    security desks, and noticeboards in halls of residence and students' union
    buildings.

drink-safe-about.pdf
    One page on what Drink Safe is, how reports are checked before they
    appear, and how to contact us. It is for whoever decides whether the
    posters can go up.


For a print shop
----------------

These files have 3mm bleed, so a print shop needs to print and trim them.
They will not come out right on an office printer.

drink-safe-flyer.pdf
    A5, double sided, with the safety tips and who can help. For welfare desks
    and stalls.

drink-safe-sticker-a.pdf
    100mm square, for the back of toilet cubicle doors. What to do if a drink
    has been spiked.

drink-safe-sticker-b.pdf
    100mm square, for mirrors and hand dryers. A QR code to check a venue.


For social media
----------------

drink-safe-reel.mp4
    A short vertical video for Instagram, TikTok or Facebook.

share-copy.txt
    Text to post with the video. Use it as it is or change it to suit.
